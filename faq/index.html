Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our notes and services

About Free Notes

Are the notes really free?

Yes! All exercise solutions are available to read online for free. You can access MCQs, short questions, long questions, and numerical problems without any payment or registration.

Which subjects are available?

We currently offer complete notes for Class 9 Physics (9 chapters), Chemistry (19 chapters), and Computer Science (7 chapters). Class 10 notes are coming soon.

Are these notes aligned with my board?

Yes! Our notes follow the National Book Foundation (NBF) syllabus used by FBISE, Punjab Board, and all major educational boards across Pakistan.

Do I need to create an account?

No registration required. Just visit the website and start reading notes immediately.

Premium Notes

What's the difference between free and premium notes?

Free notes contain exercise solutions only. Premium notes include detailed theory, SLO-based content, solved examples, diagrams, and exam preparation tips.

Can I see a sample before buying?

Yes! We offer one free sample chapter for each subject. Contact us on WhatsApp to get your free sample.

How much do premium notes cost?

Pricing varies by subject and package. Contact us on WhatsApp for current pricing and package details.

Online Tutoring

Yes — we offer 1-to-1 online tutoring for all subjects we cover. We have subject-specific teachers (for Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, and others) and can match you with the right instructor. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss availability, scheduling, and exact rates for your subject.

Can I choose my session timings?
