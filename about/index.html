OUR STORY

Built by a Student.
Run for Every Student.

The mission is simple: stop wasting students' time on irrelevant notes. Give them clear, exam-focused solutions and real tutoring via WhatsApp.

Muhammad Moqeet

Founder & Lead Educator

Teacher, tutor, and lifelong student of the NBF curriculum. Based in Pakistan, serving students across the country via WhatsApp and moqeet.com. I built Moqeet Academy because I remember the frustration of wasting hours on the wrong notes before every exam.

The Student Who Was Wasting Time

I still remember sitting at my desk the night before an exam, surrounded by notes I had downloaded from every website I could find. Pages and pages of them. Theory copied word for word from the textbook, information I had already read three times, random content that had nothing to do with the actual exam questions.

I was studying hard. But I was not studying smart. Every hour I spent reading irrelevant notes was an hour I was not spending on what actually mattered — the exercise questions, the definitions, the numericals that always showed up in the paper.

I was not alone. Every student around me was doing the same thing. We were all busy, but we were busy with the wrong things. Our notes were not helping us — they were drowning us.

I was studying for hours every night and still felt unprepared. That is when I realised the problem was not my effort — it was the notes I was using.
— Muhammad Moqeet, Founder

The Teacher Who Saw the Same Problem

Years later, I became a teacher. And on my very first day of tutoring, I saw the exact same pattern playing out in front of me. My students were struggling — not because they were not working hard, but because the notes available to them were completely wrong for how exams actually work.

Some notes were too long, with pages of theory that the board examiner never asks. Some were too vague, skipping the solved numericals entirely. Many were simply copied from textbooks with no added value at all. Others had solutions to exercises from completely different editions or different curricula — not even the NBF syllabus that Punjab Board and FBISE students actually need.

Students were downloading files from random Facebook groups, WhatsApp forwards, and sketchy websites. Nobody knew if the solutions were correct. Nobody checked. And students were going into exams trusting notes that had never been verified by anyone who actually understood the subject.

I had spent years learning from those mistakes as a student. Now as a teacher, I could not stand watching my students make the same ones.

The Decision That Started Everything

So I made a decision. I was going to build something different. Not another collection of copied textbook pages. Not another site stuffed with irrelevant theory. I was going to create notes that do exactly what notes are supposed to do — help students get marks in exams, efficiently, without wasting a single hour.

The free notes on Moqeet Academy contain only what you actually need for exercises: MCQs with correct answers, short questions answered clearly, long questions with full explanations, and numericals solved step by step. No filler. No fluff. Every word earns its place on the page.

And for students who want to go deeper — who want to understand every SLO, every concept, every detail their teacher might ask about — that is what the premium notes are for. Full chapter theory. Extra questions from inside the chapter. Exam tips. Common mistakes to avoid. Everything a serious student needs to walk into the exam hall with real confidence.

What We Believe

Clarity over quantity

One perfectly solved numerical is worth more than ten pages of copied textbook theory. We believe in giving students exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.

NBF syllabus — aligned for every Pakistani student

Our notes are built for the National Book Foundation curriculum — which means they work for FBISE students in Islamabad, Punjab Board students, and any school across Pakistan following NBF textbooks.

Free access as a right, not a privilege

Exercise solutions are and will always remain free on this website. Every student in Pakistan deserves access to correct solutions, regardless of what they can afford.

Personal connection through WhatsApp

We are not a faceless platform. When you message on WhatsApp, a real person replies — the same teacher who wrote your notes and who genuinely wants you to succeed in your exam.

SLO-based premium notes built for real exams

FBISE and Punjab Board exams are tested against Student Learning Outcomes. Our premium notes are structured around each SLO so students never miss a point that the examiner could award marks for.

Why WhatsApp, Not a Fancy App

You might wonder — why WhatsApp? Why not a proper app with payments and downloads?

Because I know how Pakistani students actually live. Not everyone has a credit card. Not everyone's parents are comfortable with online payment portals they have never heard of. But almost every student in Pakistan has WhatsApp. EasyPaisa and JazzCash work everywhere. And a real person replying to your message is more trustworthy than any automated checkout page.

The small fee for the PDF is not really about the money. It filters serious students from those who are just collecting files they will never open. Every student who pays even a small amount is more likely to actually read the notes. And as a teacher, that matters to me far more than the transaction itself.
