Class 9 Chemistry Chapter 13 Notes — Hydrocarbons and Functional Groups NBF Syllabus

Chapter Overview: Chapter 13 introduces fundamental concepts of Organic Chemistry under the NBF Syllabus. It details condensed structural representations, standard naming stems like Butane frameworks, saturated versus unsaturated double configurations, structural oxygen links, and chemical properties defined by active functional centers.

Key Topics in This Chapter

  • Molecular, structural, and condensed structural formulas of hydrocarbons
  • Saturated vs. unsaturated hydrocarbons (alkanes, alkenes, alkynes)
  • The chemical concept of a functional group and structural classification
  • Identifying organic groups: Alcohols, Aldehydes, Ketones, Esters, and Ethers

Short Response Questions

Q3. Identify the functional groups present in the given structural representations:

  • $CH_3–CH_2–OH$: Contains the hydroxyl group ($–OH$). Functional group: Alcohol
  • $CH_3–COOH$: Contains the carboxyl group ($–COOH$). Functional group: Carboxylic acid
  • $CH_3–CO–CH_3$: Contains the carbonyl group ($C=O$) bounded by two carbon chains. Functional group: Ketone
  • $CH_3–COO–CH_3$: Contains the ester link ($–COO–$). Functional group: Ester
  • $H–CHO$: Contains the formyl aldehyde group ($–CHO$). Functional group: Aldehyde

Q4. What is the name of the alkane having four carbon atoms in the chain?

The straight-chain alkane containing four carbon atoms is called Butane. Its molecular formula is $C_4H_{10}$ and its condensed structure is written as $CH_3–CH_2–CH_2–CH_3$.

Long Response Questions

Q5. Give the structural formula of two simple alkanes and one alkyne.

Alkanes (Saturated hydrocarbons containing only single covalent carbon bonds):

1. Methane ($CH_4$):

H | H — C — H | H

2. Ethane ($C_2H_6$ or $CH_3–CH_3$):

H H | | H — C — C — H | | H H

Alkyne (Unsaturated hydrocarbon containing a triple covalent carbon bond):

1. Ethyne / Acetylene ($C_2H_2$):

H — C ≡ C — H

Q6. What is meant by the term functional group? Explain with examples.

A functional group is an atom or a specific arrangement of atoms within an organic molecule that determines its characteristic chemical properties and reactions. Organic compounds are organized into classes based on their active functional blocks.

Examples:

  • Hydroxyl Group ($–OH$): Defines the alcohol family. Example: Ethanol ($CH_3–CH_2–OH$).
  • Carboxyl Group ($–COOH$): Defines the carboxylic acid family. Example: Acetic acid ($CH_3–COOH$).
  • Aldehyde Group ($–CHO$): Formed when a carbonyl group is bonded to at least one hydrogen atom. Example: Formaldehyde ($HCHO$).

Q7. Identify the type of the following compounds as an alcohol, aldehyde, or ketone:

Based on structural properties, the given compounds are classified as follows:

  • (a) $HCHO$: Contains the structural aldehyde unit ($–CHO$). Therefore, it belongs to the Aldehyde class (Formaldehyde).
  • (b) $CH_3COCH_3$: Contains a central carbonyl unit ($C=O$) placed between two separate carbon rings. Therefore, it belongs to the Ketone class (Acetone).
  • (c) $CH_3CH_2OH$: Contains an end hydroxyl chain component ($–OH$). Therefore, it belongs to the Alcohol class (Ethanol).

⭐ Exam Tips for Chapter 13

When drawing structural formulas, ensure that every Carbon atom always shows exactly four bonds, Oxygen shows two bonds, and Hydrogen shows a single bond. Double check whether a carbonyl unit represents an aldehyde or a ketone by checking what atoms are connected directly to the $C=O$ carbon atom!
