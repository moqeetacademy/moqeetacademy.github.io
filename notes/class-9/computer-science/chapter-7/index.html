Class 9 Computer Science Chapter 7 Notes — Entrepreneurship NBF Syllabus

Unit Overview: This chapter introduces the concept of entrepreneurship, the roles and characteristics of an entrepreneur, the types of entrepreneurs, and the impact of entrepreneurship on the economy. Students will explore how entrepreneurs contribute to economic growth, reduce unemployment, and drive innovation through business ventures.

Key Topics Covered

  • Functions and Characteristics of an Entrepreneur
  • Types of Entrepreneurs (Technical, Innovative, Novice)
  • Role of Entrepreneurship in Economic Development
  • Advantages and Disadvantages of Owning a Business
  • Business Planning and Market Research
  • Contributions of Small Businesses to the Economy
  • Youth Entrepreneurship and Digital Innovation

Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. Which are the functions of an entrepreneur?
  • A. Risk bearing
  • B. Innovation
  • C. Organizing and managing
  • D. A, B, C
Correct Answer: D. A, B, C
Explanation: An entrepreneur performs multiple key functions — they bear financial and business risks, introduce innovation into the market, and also organize and manage resources effectively to run a successful business.
Q2. An entrepreneur who enters into business to improve quality products by their inventions and discoveries is known as:
  • A. Technical entrepreneur
  • B. Professional entrepreneur
  • C. Novice Entrepreneur
  • D. A, B, C
Correct Answer: A. Technical entrepreneur
Explanation: A technical entrepreneur relies on scientific knowledge, inventions, and discoveries to improve product quality and develop new technologies. They focus on research-based innovation rather than market trends alone.
Q3. An entrepreneur who introduces new ideas, methods of production, and discovers new markets is called:
  • A. Technical entrepreneur
  • B. Innovative Entrepreneur
  • C. Novice Entrepreneur
  • D. A, B, C
Correct Answer: B. Innovative Entrepreneur
Explanation: An innovative entrepreneur brings fresh ideas, introduces new production methods, and explores untapped markets. They are change-makers who drive progress through creativity and forward-thinking strategies.
Q4. Entrepreneurship can ______ the Gross National Product.
  • A. Increase
  • B. Decrease
  • C. Neither increase nor decrease
  • D. None of A, B, C
Correct Answer: A. Increase
Explanation: Entrepreneurship contributes to economic activity by creating new businesses, products, and services. This increases overall production and output in the economy, which directly raises the Gross National Product (GNP).
Q5. Entrepreneurship can ______ the Unemployment.
  • A. Increase
  • B. Neither increase nor decrease
  • C. Decrease
  • D. A, B, C
Correct Answer: C. Decrease
Explanation: When entrepreneurs start new businesses, they create jobs and employment opportunities for others in the community. This directly helps in reducing the unemployment rate in the economy.
Q6. A stable and dynamic political environment plays:
  • A. Positive role in business growth
  • B. Negative role in business growth
  • C. No role in business growth
  • D. None of A, B, C
Correct Answer: A. Positive role in business growth
Explanation: A stable and dynamic political environment builds investor confidence, ensures legal protection, and provides a consistent policy framework — all of which encourage businesses to start, grow, and thrive.
Q7. A business plan is a written summary of:
  • A. Proposed business venture
  • B. Its operational and financial details
  • C. Its marketing opportunities and strategy
  • D. A, B, C
Correct Answer: D. A, B, C
Explanation: A comprehensive business plan covers all essential aspects of a venture — the business idea itself, the operational and financial planning, and the marketing strategies needed to attract customers and grow the business.

Also be prepared to explain how entrepreneurship contributes to the national economy — especially its role in reducing unemployment and increasing GNP. Long questions require well-structured answers with clear advantages/disadvantages.

Long Questions

Q1. How well does the entrepreneur fit the entrepreneurial profile described in this chapter?

An entrepreneur fits the entrepreneurial profile because they possess qualities that help them start and manage a business successfully. They take responsibility for their decisions, are willing to take moderate risks, and have confidence in their abilities.

Entrepreneurs are highly motivated to achieve success, seek feedback to improve performance, and work with great energy and dedication. They are also skilled at organizing resources and overcoming challenges. These characteristics enable them to grow and sustain their businesses effectively.

Q2. Interpret the advantages and disadvantages that the entrepreneur sees in owning a business.

Owning a business offers both benefits and challenges.

Advantages: Entrepreneurs enjoy independence and control over business decisions. They have the opportunity to earn higher profits and can work according to their own schedule. Business ownership also allows them to express creativity, pursue their passions, and develop valuable skills.

Disadvantages: Running a business involves financial risk and uncertainty. Entrepreneurs often face stress, long working hours, and heavy responsibilities. Balancing work and personal life can also be difficult.

Therefore, while business ownership can be rewarding, it requires commitment and hard work.

Q3. Conclude the advice that could be offered to someone who is considering launching a business.

A person planning to launch a business should begin with a clear business plan and conduct thorough market research. They should understand the financial risks involved and prepare strategies to manage them.

It is important to stay flexible and adapt to changing market conditions. Hard work, commitment, and customer satisfaction are essential for success. Seeking advice from experienced entrepreneurs and ensuring compliance with legal requirements can also help avoid common mistakes.

With proper planning and determination, a business can achieve long-term success.

Q4. Articulate the main reason for young people to be involved in business.

Young people are encouraged to become involved in business because it provides opportunities for independence, creativity, and financial growth. Entrepreneurship allows them to turn their ideas into reality and make their own decisions.

Many young people also want to create a positive impact on society through innovative products and services. In addition, their familiarity with technology and digital platforms helps them build and expand businesses more effectively. These factors make entrepreneurship an attractive career option for young people.

Q5. What are the contributions that small businesses make to our economy?

Small businesses play a vital role in economic development. They create employment opportunities and help reduce unemployment in local communities. Small businesses encourage innovation by introducing new products, services, and ideas.

They support local economies by providing goods and services and participating in community activities. In addition, they increase competition, giving consumers more choices and better prices. Small businesses also promote entrepreneurship and wealth creation, which contributes to overall economic growth and stability.
